# Grok Imagine prompts for SARbit (no alien)

The alien stays as he is. These prompts are for **everything around him**: animations first, then still images.

## Rules before you generate (Space Apps)

- **Every AI image or video needs a visible watermark** (for example a small "AI-generated · Grok Imagine" corner tag). Add it
  when you cut the file, and list it in the AI disclosure.
- **No NASA logos, flags or mission marks** in anything generated. Draw "a radar satellite", never "the NASA NISAR satellite with logo".
- **No people at all**, and above all no children: the rules ban likenesses of anyone under 18, and AI people look off anyway.
- Keep the game's look. Paste this **style line** at the end of every prompt:

> *Style: cute cartoon sticker art, thick clean outlines, soft cel shading, bright mint green (#7cf3c7), warm gold (#ffc24a)
> and coral red (#ff5a5a) accents on a deep navy space background (#0b1020), friendly and simple, readable at small size,
> no text, no logos, no people.*

For transparent cut-outs, ask for "isolated on a plain flat dark navy background" and cut it out, as with the alien art.

### Tips that worked for the current art

- Generate 3–4 versions and keep the one with the cleanest outline; segment-export, then shrink to WebP (≤ 100 KB per icon).
- Ask for "isolated, centred, lots of padding" for icons so they cut out cleanly.
- For videos, ask for "seamless loop" and "static camera" on anything that must sit behind the UI; motion behind text is hard to read.

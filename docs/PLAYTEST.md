# SARbit playtest (10 minutes, one kid)

**Goal:** find out where a kid gets stuck, bored or confused. Not to teach them, not to defend the game.

## Before

- Open **https://sarbit-three.vercel.app/#playtest** on the device the kid will use (tablet or laptop). Sound on.
- The `#playtest` part makes the game keep short notes on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- If the kid played before: Finish → Play again (clears stars). Have a phone ready to film the screen + hands if
  the parent agrees, or just take notes.

## What you say (only this)

> "This is a game about an alien with a radar. Play it the way you want. Say out loud what you're thinking.
> I can't help you, but I'm curious what you do."

Then be quiet. If they're stuck for **30 seconds**, say: "What do you think you should do?" Only after another
30 seconds give one small hint, and write down that you did.

Let them play **2–3 missions**. Suggested order: **Limpopo floods** (easy), **Clearing along BR-163** (needs the
🌿 glasses), then one they pick.

## What to watch (tick or write a word)

| Moment | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Intro | Reads the text? Types a name without help? |
| World map | Knows to tap a pin or the list? Which mission do they pick and why? |
| 👀 Look | Finds and moves the slider? Says what changed? |
| ✨ Glow | Drags or taps the cards? Understands red / blue-green? Uses the radar glasses? Reads the alien's bubble? |
| 🎯 Find | Taps glowing places or random ones? Reacts to "+150" / hearts / sounds? Uses 💡 Hint? Gives up? |
| ⭐ Score | Looks at the stars? Wants to play again or move on? |
| Anywhere | Taps the "?" buttons? Laughs, sighs, says "boring", "cool", "what?" |

## After (ask exactly these)

1. What was this game about?
2. What do the colours red and blue-green mean?
3. What was the most fun part? The most annoying part?
4. Would you play another mission? Why / why not?

## Send back

On the score screen press **📋 Copy playtest notes** and paste it in the team chat, together with your notes and
the kid's answers (age, device). One line per mission looks like:

```
12:07:03 A3: 2★ 1145pts, found 5/8, taps hit 5 miss 1 tiny 0 again 0, hints 1, glasses switches 1 (best yes),
drops 2, time look 1.6s, glow 2.8s, find 2.4s, hearts left 2
```

What we'll change from it: anything where the kid was stuck > 30 s, anything they misunderstood in question 2,
and whatever they called boring.
